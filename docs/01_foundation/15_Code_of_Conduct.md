# Purpose

This document defines the official Code of Conduct for Limoxel.

Its purpose is to establish the standards of professionalism, respect, collaboration, and integrity expected from everyone participating in the Limoxel community.

A healthy engineering community is built not only through high-quality software, but also through respectful communication, responsible collaboration, and shared commitment to long-term success.

As a Core Foundation Document, this document serves as the canonical source of truth for community conduct throughout the lifecycle of Limoxel.

---

# Code of Conduct

Limoxel is committed to building a professional, respectful, transparent, and collaborative engineering community where ideas are evaluated on technical merit, contributors are treated with dignity, and constructive discussion leads to better software.

Every participant shares responsibility for maintaining an environment that reflects the same quality, integrity, and professionalism expected of the platform itself.

---

# Conduct Principles

## 1. Respect Above All

Every participant deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.

Differences in experience, background, or opinion should never become reasons for disrespectful behavior.

Professionalism is expected in every interaction.

---

## 2. Ideas Over Individuals

Engineering discussions should focus on technical merit rather than personal identity.

Ideas should be evaluated based on evidence, reasoning, architectural consistency, and long-term engineering value—not on seniority, popularity, employer, or reputation.

The strongest engineering solution should prevail.

---

## 3. Professional Communication

Communication should always remain:

- Respectful
- Professional
- Constructive
- Honest
- Solution-oriented

Criticism should address the implementation or proposal, never the individual.

---

## 4. Inclusive Collaboration

Limoxel welcomes contributors from diverse backgrounds, experience levels, industries, organizations, and regions.

Everyone should have the opportunity to participate in engineering discussions and contribute meaningfully within the project's established standards.

---

## 5. Transparency

Engineering discussions, architectural decisions, repository governance, and community policies should remain transparent whenever practical.

Clear communication strengthens trust throughout the project.

---

## 6. Assume Good Intent

Participants should begin discussions by assuming others are acting in good faith.

Questions should be encouraged, misunderstandings clarified, and conclusions reached only after thoughtful discussion.

---

## 7. Foster a Learning Environment

Limoxel values continuous learning.

Questions are encouraged.

Knowledge should be shared openly.

Code reviews, documentation, and technical discussions should help contributors become stronger engineers.

The community succeeds when its members continue to grow.

---

## 8. Constructive Technical Disagreement

Healthy disagreement is an essential part of engineering.

Architectural debates, design discussions, and implementation reviews should be supported by evidence, experimentation, benchmarks, engineering principles, and technical reasoning.

Personal conflict should never replace technical discussion.

---

## 9. Shared Responsibility

Every participant contributes to the quality of the community.

Maintainers, contributors, reviewers, and users all share responsibility for preserving professionalism, collaboration, and engineering excellence.

---

## 10. Integrity

Engineering integrity begins with honesty.

Participants should openly acknowledge:

- Bugs
- Mistakes
- Limitations
- Risks
- Uncertainty

Transparency enables better engineering decisions and strengthens trust throughout the project.

---

## 11. Recognition

Every meaningful contribution deserves appreciation.

Whether someone contributes:

- Code
- Documentation
- Testing
- Reviews
- Design
- Bug reports
- Discussions
- Community support

their effort helps strengthen Limoxel and should be recognized with respect and gratitude.

---

## 12. Protect the Community

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, abusive behavior, personal attacks, or any form of hostile conduct have no place within Limoxel.

Maintainers are responsible for protecting the community and preserving a safe, respectful, and professional environment for everyone.

---

## 13. Long-Term Relationships

Interactions should be approached as long-term professional relationships rather than short-term transactions.

Respectful communities attract thoughtful contributors and produce better engineering outcomes.

---

## 14. Community Before Ego

The long-term success of Limoxel is more important than individual recognition.

Collaboration, shared learning, and engineering excellence should always take precedence over personal pride.

---

## 15. Engineering Excellence Through Collaboration

The highest-quality engineering emerges when contributors feel comfortable sharing ideas, challenging assumptions respectfully, and working together toward better solutions.

A strong engineering culture produces strong software.

---

## 16. Respect Repository Governance

Limoxel is an open-source project governed by its official repository.

Community participation is encouraged and appreciated, but repository governance exists to preserve the project's quality, stability, and long-term direction.

All participants are expected to respect engineering reviews, governance decisions, and the established contribution process, even when their proposals are not accepted.

Professional disagreement is welcome; disrespect toward governance decisions is not.

---

## 17. Protect the Reputation of the Project

Every participant represents Limoxel when interacting within its community.

Professional behavior, thoughtful communication, and responsible collaboration help preserve the credibility and reputation of both the project and its contributors.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all official Limoxel spaces, including:

- Source code repositories
- Pull requests
- Issues
- Discussions
- Documentation
- Design proposals
- Code reviews
- Community forums
- Official communication channels
- Events organized under the Limoxel project

Every participant is expected to follow these principles regardless of their role.

---

# Foundational Principles

The Code of Conduct establishes the following permanent commitments.

- Treat everyone with respect.
- Evaluate ideas based on technical merit.
- Communicate professionally and constructively.
- Encourage an inclusive engineering community.
- Promote transparency and honesty.
- Foster learning and knowledge sharing.
- Resolve disagreements through evidence and engineering reasoning.
- Recognize every meaningful contribution.
- Protect the safety and professionalism of the community.
- Respect repository governance and engineering leadership.
- Prioritize collaboration over individual recognition.
- Build a community worthy of the engineering standards Limoxel represents.

---

# Authority

This document is part of Limoxel's Core Foundation Documentation and serves as the canonical source of truth for community conduct.

All community interactions, engineering discussions, reviews, governance activities, and official communication should remain consistent with the principles established in this document.

---

# Applicability

The principles defined in this document apply throughout the entire lifecycle of Limoxel and are expected to guide:

- Community participation
- Technical discussions
- Pull requests
- Code reviews
- Documentation
- Governance
- Conflict resolution
- Community moderation
- Long-term ecosystem development

The success of the Limoxel community should be measured not only by technical achievement but also by the professionalism, integrity, and mutual respect demonstrated by its members.

---

# Change Policy

The Code of Conduct represents the enduring community philosophy of Limoxel and is intended to remain stable throughout the lifetime of the project.

Changes to this document should be exceptionally rare and only occur when the project's community governance philosophy fundamentally evolves.

Any modification requires explicit approval from the repository owner.
