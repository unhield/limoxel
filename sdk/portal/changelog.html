Semantic Versioning & Compatibility Policy

The Limoxel SDK strictly adheres to Semantic Versioning 2.0.0. Major releases represent breaking contract modifications, Minor releases represent backward-compatible capability additions, and Patch releases represent backward-compatible bug/security fixes.
