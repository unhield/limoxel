# Purpose

This document defines the official target users of Limoxel.

The purpose of identifying target users is to ensure that every product, engineering, architectural, and strategic decision is made with a clear understanding of who Limoxel is designed to serve.

As a Core Foundation Document, this document serves as the canonical source of truth for identifying the primary and secondary audiences of the project.

---

# Target Users

Limoxel is designed for engineers and organizations working with medium to large software systems where understanding, maintaining, and evolving codebases are significant engineering challenges.

The platform prioritizes users who require reliable engineering intelligence over users seeking introductory programming education or automated code generation.

---

# User Classification

## Primary Users

### Software Engineers

Professional software engineers responsible for developing, maintaining, debugging, refactoring, and evolving medium to large codebases.

These users rely on Limoxel to better understand repository structure, architecture, dependencies, and system behavior before making engineering decisions.

---

### Engineering Teams

Development teams collaborating on shared repositories that require consistent architectural understanding, impact analysis, and engineering knowledge across multiple contributors.

Limoxel helps establish a shared understanding of complex software systems.

---

### Open-Source Maintainers

Maintainers responsible for reviewing contributions, preserving architectural consistency, onboarding contributors, and ensuring the long-term health of open-source projects.

Limoxel provides reliable engineering intelligence that supports informed project governance.

---

### Software Architects

Architects responsible for designing, evaluating, and evolving software systems over long periods of time.

Limoxel assists in understanding system structure, architectural boundaries, dependencies, and evolution strategies.

---

### AI-Assisted Developers

Engineers using AI-powered development tools who require verified repository knowledge to improve reasoning quality, reduce hallucinations, and increase confidence in AI-assisted software development.

Limoxel serves as trusted engineering infrastructure rather than an autonomous coding agent.

---

## Secondary Users

### Platform Engineering Teams

Teams responsible for maintaining shared engineering platforms, internal developer tooling, and organizational software infrastructure.

---

### DevOps and Infrastructure Engineers

Engineers managing deployment pipelines, infrastructure automation, platform reliability, and operational systems who require a deeper understanding of repository relationships and engineering dependencies.

---

### Security Engineers

Security professionals performing architectural reviews, dependency analysis, repository assessments, and software risk evaluation.

---

### Engineering Researchers

Researchers exploring software engineering, program analysis, repository intelligence, software evolution, developer productivity, and AI-assisted engineering.

---

### Advanced Students

Students with a strong understanding of software engineering who wish to study the architecture, evolution, and design of real-world software systems.

Limoxel is intended as an advanced engineering platform rather than a beginner learning environment.

---

# Non-Target Users

Limoxel is not primarily designed for:

- Beginner programmers learning to write their first programs.
- Users seeking low-code or no-code application builders.
- Prompt-to-application generation workflows.
- General-purpose AI chat applications.
- Project management platforms.
- Version control platforms.
- Continuous Integration or Continuous Deployment systems.
- Repository hosting services.
- IDE replacements.
- Educational coding tutorials.

These use cases fall outside the primary objectives of Limoxel.

# Foundational Principles

The target user strategy establishes the following permanent principles.

- Build for professional engineering workflows.
- Prioritize complex software systems over small projects.
- Design for engineers before general consumers.
- Support both individual engineers and collaborative teams.
- Treat AI systems as consumers of engineering intelligence rather than replacements for engineers.
- Favor depth of capability over breadth of audience.
- Focus on solving real engineering problems with lasting value.
