# Purpose

This document defines the official licensing philosophy and licensing model of Limoxel.

Its purpose is to establish how the project may be used, modified, distributed, and contributed to while preserving the long-term openness, integrity, and sustainability of the platform.

As a Core Foundation Document, this document serves as the canonical source of truth for the licensing strategy of Limoxel.

---

# Licensing Statement

Limoxel is an open-source engineering intelligence platform committed to openness, transparency, collaboration, and long-term sustainability.

The project is licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**.

The Apache License 2.0 has been selected because it encourages broad adoption while providing strong legal clarity, patent protection, and commercial friendliness without compromising the open-source nature of the project.

---

# Licensing Philosophy

## 1. Open Source by Design

Limoxel is built as an open-source platform.

Its source code should remain openly available for learning, inspection, collaboration, improvement, and responsible innovation.

Open source is a fundamental characteristic of the project rather than a distribution strategy.

---

## 2. Encourage Adoption

The licensing model should minimize unnecessary barriers to adoption.

Individuals, educational institutions, researchers, startups, enterprises, and open-source communities should be able to use Limoxel confidently within the permissions granted by the project's license.

---

## 3. Encourage Innovation

Users are encouraged to study, modify, extend, and build upon Limoxel in accordance with the Apache License 2.0.

The project should serve as a foundation for future engineering innovation while preserving proper attribution and legal compliance.

---

## 4. Commercial Use Is Welcome

Commercial use of Limoxel is permitted under the terms of the Apache License 2.0.

Organizations are encouraged to integrate, extend, or build solutions around Limoxel while respecting the obligations defined by the license.

---

## 5. Patent Protection

The Apache License 2.0 provides an explicit patent grant that protects both contributors and users.

This promotes long-term confidence for organizations adopting Limoxel in production environments.

---

## 6. Community Before Ownership

Although the repository is governed by its owner, the knowledge created by Limoxel is intended to benefit the broader software engineering community.

The licensing model exists to maximize collaboration while maintaining responsible stewardship of the project.

---

## 7. Transparency

The project's licensing terms, governance documents, and engineering standards should remain publicly available.

Users should always understand the legal framework under which Limoxel is distributed.

---

## 8. Sustainable Open Source

Licensing decisions should always prioritize the long-term sustainability of the project.

Short-term commercial opportunities should never compromise the openness or engineering integrity of Limoxel.

---

## 9. Preserve Engineering Integrity

The open-source license grants freedom to use and modify the software.

However, it does not grant authority to define the project's direction, engineering standards, governance, or official releases.

The integrity of the official project is preserved through responsible repository governance.

---

## 10. Long-Term Stability

The licensing model should remain stable over the lifetime of Limoxel.

Changes to the project's license should occur only under exceptional circumstances when they clearly benefit the project's long-term future.

# Relationship Between Licensing and Governance

The Apache License 2.0 grants broad rights to use, study, modify, and distribute Limoxel.

It does **not** grant:

- Repository ownership.
- Maintainer status.
- Merge permissions.
- Governance authority.
- Release authority.
- Architectural decision-making authority.

Those responsibilities remain governed by the project's governance documents.
