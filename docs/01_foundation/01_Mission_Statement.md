# Purpose

This document defines the official mission of Limoxel.

The mission establishes the fundamental purpose of the project and serves as the highest-level guiding principle for all product, engineering, architectural, and governance decisions.

As a Core Foundation Document, this document is a canonical source of truth and is intended to remain stable throughout the lifetime of the project.

---

# Mission Statement

> **Limoxel exists to make understanding, navigating, and evolving complex software systems effortless by providing an open-source engineering intelligence platform that empowers both developers and AI to work with confidence.**

---

# Mission Interpretation

The mission establishes five permanent commitments that define the direction of Limoxel.

## 1. Simplify Software Understanding

Modern software systems continue to increase in scale and complexity. Limoxel exists to reduce the cognitive effort required to understand these systems by transforming repositories into structured, reliable engineering intelligence.

---

## 2. Enable Confident Software Evolution

Understanding software is only the beginning. Limoxel enables engineers to analyze, maintain, extend, and evolve complex systems with greater confidence through verified engineering knowledge and trustworthy insights.

---

## 3. Empower Both Humans and AI

Limoxel is designed as engineering infrastructure for both software engineers and AI systems. Rather than replacing engineering expertise, it provides a shared knowledge foundation that enables more accurate reasoning, better decision-making, and safer software evolution.

---

## 4. Build Open Engineering Infrastructure

Limoxel is committed to building an open-source engineering intelligence platform that promotes transparency, collaboration, knowledge sharing, and long-term community participation while maintaining uncompromising engineering standards.

---

## 5. Earn Trust Through Engineering Excellence

Trust is the foundation of Limoxel.

Every capability developed within the platform should strengthen developer confidence through correctness, reliability, transparency, maintainability, and thoughtful engineering. Long-term trust will always take precedence over rapid feature expansion.

---

# Scope

The mission defined in this document applies to every aspect of Limoxel, including but not limited to:

- Product strategy
- System architecture
- Engineering practices
- Feature development
- Public APIs
- Plugin ecosystem
- Documentation
- Repository governance
- Community collaboration
- Long-term roadmap

No project decision should intentionally conflict with the mission defined in this document.

---

# Foundational Principles

The mission establishes the following permanent principles for Limoxel.

- Solve meaningful engineering problems.
- Prioritize long-term value over short-term convenience.
- Make repository understanding effortless.
- Build on verified engineering knowledge rather than assumptions.
- Design equally for software engineers and AI systems.
- Keep the platform open, transparent, and extensible.
- Value correctness, reliability, maintainability, and trust above feature quantity.
