# Purpose

This document defines the official non-goals of Limoxel.

The purpose of identifying non-goals is to establish clear boundaries around the project's scope, ensuring that engineering effort remains focused on the problems Limoxel is designed to solve while avoiding unnecessary expansion into unrelated domains.

As a Core Foundation Document, this document serves as the canonical source of truth for what Limoxel intentionally does not aim to become.

---

# Non-Goals Statement

Limoxel is an engineering intelligence platform.

Its objective is to help software engineers and AI systems understand, navigate, maintain, and evolve complex software systems through verified engineering knowledge.

Any capability that does not directly support this objective falls outside the intended scope of the project.

---

# Core Non-Goals

## 1. Autonomous Coding Agent

Limoxel is not designed to autonomously write, modify, or maintain software on behalf of developers.

It provides engineering intelligence that supports informed decision-making rather than replacing engineering judgment.

---

## 2. AI Code Generation Platform

Limoxel is not a prompt-to-code or code generation platform.

While AI systems may consume Limoxel's engineering intelligence, generating source code is not a primary objective of the platform.

---

## 3. Integrated Development Environment (IDE)

Limoxel is not intended to replace code editors or integrated development environments.

Instead, it is designed to complement existing development tools by providing repository-aware engineering intelligence.

---

## 4. Version Control System

Limoxel is not a source code management or version control platform.

Repository hosting, branching strategies, merge management, and source control remain the responsibility of dedicated version control systems.

---

## 5. Repository Hosting Platform

Limoxel is not intended to compete with repository hosting services.

Its purpose is to understand repositories, not host or manage them.

---

## 6. Build System or CI/CD Platform

Limoxel is not responsible for building, testing, packaging, deploying, or operating software.

Continuous Integration, Continuous Deployment, and infrastructure automation remain outside the scope of the project.

---

## 7. Project Management Platform

Limoxel is not a task management, issue tracking, sprint planning, documentation management, or project collaboration platform.

Engineering intelligence should complement project management tools rather than replace them.

---

## 8. Software Testing Framework

Limoxel is not a testing framework or quality assurance platform.

Although engineering intelligence may assist testing activities, executing or managing software tests is outside the project's scope.

---

## 9. General-Purpose Search Engine

Limoxel is not designed to index or search arbitrary information on the internet.

Its intelligence is repository-centric and focused exclusively on engineering knowledge.

---

## 10. Conversational AI or Chatbot

Limoxel is not intended to become a general-purpose conversational assistant.

Natural language interfaces may exist, but they are interfaces to engineering intelligence rather than standalone chatbot products.

---

## 11. Beginner Programming Education Platform

Limoxel is not designed as an introductory programming education platform.

While developers may learn from the engineering knowledge it provides, teaching programming fundamentals is not one of its objectives.

---

## 12. Analytics Dashboard Product

Limoxel is not intended to become a dashboard-focused analytics platform centered on metrics, charts, or business reporting.

Visualization exists only when it improves engineering understanding and decision-making.

---

# Design Philosophy

Limoxel intentionally remains focused on engineering intelligence.

Features should be evaluated based on a single question:

> **Does this capability meaningfully improve the understanding, navigation, maintenance, or evolution of complex software systems?**

If the answer is no, the capability should generally be considered outside the scope of the project.

Maintaining a clear product focus is essential to preserving long-term quality, simplicity, and engineering excellence.

---

# Scope

The non-goals defined in this document influence:

- Product strategy
- Feature evaluation
- Engineering priorities
- Research direction
- Architecture decisions
- Plugin ecosystem
- Community proposals
- Long-term roadmap

Proposed features that conflict with these boundaries should be carefully evaluated before being accepted into the project.

---

# Foundational Principles

The non-goals establish the following permanent principles.

- Maintain a clear and focused product identity.
- Avoid unnecessary expansion into unrelated domains.
- Prioritize engineering intelligence over feature breadth.
- Complement existing engineering tools instead of replacing them.
- Build infrastructure rather than applications.
- Preserve long-term architectural simplicity.
- Reject features that dilute the project's mission or vision.

# Authority

This document is part of Limoxel's Core Foundation Documentation and serves as the canonical source of truth for defining the boundaries of the project.

Product planning, engineering decisions, community proposals, and roadmap discussions should remain consistent with the limitations established by this document.

---

# Applicability

The principles defined in this document apply throughout the entire lifecycle of Limoxel and are expected to guide:

- Product planning
- Feature evaluation
- Engineering decisions
- Architecture design
- Research initiatives
- Plugin development
- Community contributions
- Long-term strategic planning

Capabilities that fall outside these boundaries should not become part of Limoxel unless the project's long-term direction fundamentally changes.

---

# Change Policy

The non-goals define the intended boundaries of Limoxel and are expected to remain stable throughout the lifetime of the project.

Changes to this document should be exceptionally rare and only occur when the long-term purpose or strategic direction of Limoxel fundamentally evolves.

Any modification requires explicit approval from the repository owner.
